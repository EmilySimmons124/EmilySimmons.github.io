# Design Goals and Base Design:

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; This pickleball paddle was custom-designed for my experience level. The last time I played pickleball was in 2019 in middle school PE class. I am considering myself a complete beginner. I have, however, taken tennis lessons at several points in my life. My main objective for the paddle was to be easy to move and make quick adjustments. I was less conservative with the power of my swing, as I will primarily focus on my swing and making sure the ball gets over the net.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; In addition to being light, I made the paddle 8.5" wide. A paddle can only be 24" wide plus the length (including the handle). This means that my paddle is only slightly taller than it is wide. This limits my lateral range when playing, but being able to hit the ball is more important than the range at my level. The added width means I will not have to be as precise about how high the racket is when swinging, which should make it easier to hit even if I don't judge the ball's height correctly. 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; I wanted to keep my paddle light so it would be easy for me to move it when hitting the ball. The paddle weighs only *****, which is considered lightweight for a paddle. This will reduce the power of my swing but make it significantly easier to make last-minute adjustments.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;  Despite being lightweight, my paddle has a center of mass around 8.5" from the base of the handle. This is slightly below the midpoint of the paddle face. This will slightly improve the power of my swing. This comes at the cost of increased muscular load on the small forearm muscles. However, because of my tennis-playing history, reducing additional strain was not a design goal. 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The combination of lightweight and a center of mass away from the handle will give me the optimal balance of easy, quick movement while maintaining some power behind the hits. 
   
# Additional Features:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; This paddle also includes an interchangeable edge guard to protect the paddle's face. The edge guard will protect the face of the paddle outside the matching area. It is also press-fit around the edge of the paddle, making it easy to replace when it gets damaged without risk of coming off while playing. In addition to protecting the face of the paddle, it allows easy modification of the paddle.
![My Image](/EmilySimmons.github.io/assets/img/pickleball/Pickleball_edge_guards.jpg){: width="40%" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"}

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; There is also a removable cap at the bottom of the paddle handle that allows further customization and protection against damage. Often, the bottom of the handle takes the largest amount of damage outside of a game, and thus being able to change it easily prolongs the life of the paddle. Similar to the edge guard, it is a press-fit around the paddle handle, with an additional two pegs that go into the handle to provide extra security.

# Printing Technology:
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Fused filament fabrication (FFF) was used to manufacture the pickleball paddle. FFF was used because it allowed quick, inexpensive fabrication while providing freedom in the material and infill used. Resin and pSLS printing would both be unsuitable for manufacturing the paddle, as resin printing would be very brittle, and pSLS has fewer print settings, limiting the ability to adjust the infill percent to adjust weight. 

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; The paddle was printed in four parts: the face, the handle, the handle cap, and the edge guard. All components were printed with 15% rectangular infill, except for the paddle face, which had 20% infill. The additional infill on the paddle face is to improve the strength, as it will take the majority of the force in a match. The paddle face and handle were printed in PET-G due to its strength. The edge guard and handle cap were printed in PLA, as they are purely cosmetic and easy to replace if they get damaged.
