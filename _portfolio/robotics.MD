# The Competition

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is a robotics competition for high school students. In the 2024 season, robots had to shoot "notes" into a "speaker" and climb on a chain, both autonomously and while being controlled by a driver. While we have mentors, our robot is completely student designed, manufactured, and built.

![My Image](/EmilySimmons.github.io/assets/img/robotics/robot_cad.png){: width="40%" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"}

# Our Design

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Our robot consisted of a swerve drivetrain (which allows the robot to move in any direction without having to turn), an intake to pick notes up from the floor, a shooter that spins ~6500 rpm, and a climber that allowed us to climb in less than 3 seconds. It also included a hook that prevented us from sliding on the chain. The robot had 8 sensors, including beam-breaks, limelight, and cameras, used to track robot position, auto alignment, and note detection/intaking.

![My Image](/EmilySimmons.github.io/assets/img/robotics/robot_climb.jpg){: width="60%" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"}

# My Position

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; While working in almost every subgroup on the team, my main focuses for this season were electrical, mechanical, and manufacturing. Using a ShopBot CNC, I custom-cut all the parts for our robot as well as assembling and wiring them to FRC standards.

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; During competition I was part of the Drive Team (a group of 5 people on the field interacting with and controlling the robot), and I worked on repairing the robot between matches.

![My Image](/EmilySimmons.github.io/assets/img/robotics/robot_field.jpg){: width="60%" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto;"}

# Why Robotics

&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Beyond learning engineering and having fun, robotics teaches me how to work and be a leader in a large group. I have also had the opportunity to work on parts of the team that do not directly build the robot. Through robotics, I have learned how to write grant applications and have been able to direct and participate in engineering outreach events.
